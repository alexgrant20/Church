# Church management software and church apps in Asia (priority: Indonesia)

Research date: 2026-10-27. About 16 searches and fetches. Most product facts come from vendor marketing pages or app-store listings that were relayed through aggregators. No independent reviews were verified. Google Play pages could not be fetched (the content came back truncated), so this research has **no first-hand Play Store review text**.

## 1. Which church apps and ChMS products exist in Indonesia, and what do they offer?

### Takeaway
Indonesia has three tiers of products. The first is small local SaaS ChMS products sold in rupiah per month (Gerejaku.id, e-Gereja, GerejaSoft, gerejaDigital.id). The second is agency-built, white-label member apps for large churches (Shiftsoft for several GBI churches; in-house or contracted apps for GMS, GBI Sinode and GRII Pusat). The third is a long tail of single-congregation web systems built as university theses. Many congregations still keep records in Excel, Word or paper.

### Cited Findings
**Local SaaS ChMS products**
- **Gerejaku.id**, by CV. ASFA Solution of Cirebon. The company has operated since 2013, and the product launched on 1 Sep 2023.
  - Individual records cover Klasis/Sektor, contact details, citizenship, education, work, marriage and atestasi.
  - "Kartu Keluarga Jemaat" records the marriage, the head of household and family members, and prints family cards.
  - It tracks Pengurus, Majelis and Pendeta by term and by commission or function.
  - Reports cover birthdays, wedding anniversaries and member counts by category.
  - Admins can rename the hierarchy terms (klasis/sektor/md/mupel).
  - Pricing per month: Free up to 100 members; Starter Rp119.000 up to 250; Advanced Rp229.000 up to 500; Pro Rp359.000 above 500. A 30-day trial is offered.
  - The client logos shown are GBIAWN, GKI, GPDI, Tiberias, GSKI and GIDI.
  - The page does not mention NIK, baptism or sidi fields, WhatsApp, or export. — [Gerejaku.id](https://gerejaku.id/)
- **e-Gereja.com**, a cloud ChMS.
  - Modules: membership, finance and accounting (offerings, cash and general journals), e-bulletin and digital order of service, budgeting, committee and event management, asset management, and multi-user access with configurable rights.
  - Tiers: Basic is free up to 50 members. Professional is Rp150.000/month (list price Rp250.000). Enterprise is Rp500.000/month and adds assets, a custom domain, committees and a member mobile app.
  - The page gives no client names and no security detail beyond calling the system "aman" (secure). — [e-Gereja](https://www.e-gereja.com/)
- **GerejaSoft** ("CORE" church growth software).
  - Modules: a web app reachable over the church LAN or the internet; a mobile web app for ministry heads; Android/iOS apps; attendance by QR code, RFID or biometrics; online giving through a payment gateway; Gereja TV streaming; event and seminar reservations; online Bible study; and automatic ministry scheduling over WhatsApp with reminders.
  - It supports custom fields and prints "KAJ, KKJ" (Kartu Anggota Jemaat / Kartu Keluarga Jemaat) and certificates. — [GerejaSoft about](https://www.gerejasoft.com/about.php)
  - A search snippet said 800+ churches have used it since 2011. That figure is the vendor's own claim and was not visible on the fetched page. — [GerejaSoft developer](https://www.gerejasoft.com/developer.php)
- **gerejaDigital.id**: a member database, RFID attendance, management of office holders (kepengurusan), inventory, finance reports and notifications. Its site returned HTTP 500 when fetched, so these details come from the search snippet only. — [gerejaDigital.id](https://gerejadigital.id/)
- **Desktop "software database gereja"** with member-card printing is still sold or shared through blogs (2017–2019 posts; older information). — [softwaredatabasegereja blog](https://softwaredatabasegereja.blogspot.com/2017/08/download-software-database-jemaat.html), [simjemaat blog](https://simjemaat.blogspot.com/2019/06/jual-software-data-jemaat-gereja.html)

**Agency or white-label member apps**
- **Shiftsoft** builds native Android/iOS church apps.
  - Features: member data plus digital kartu jemaat; worship and komsel (cell group) attendance; warta gereja (church bulletin) with push notifications; offerings, tithes and faith pledges (janji iman); penggembalaan (pastoral follow-up and growth monitoring); ministry scheduling; activity points (poin keaktifan); asset lending; and "WA Blast", which sends announcements from the church's own WhatsApp number.
  - Giving runs through the DANA and LinkAja e-wallets.
  - Clients named: GPIA SMCC, GBI PCW Purwokerto, GBI Daan Mogot, GBI Haleluya and GBI Bethel. JKI Manahaim appears in a search snippet.
  - Offer: 2 months free. — [Shiftsoft](https://shiftsoft.id/)
- **GBI Bethel** has its own app on Play (package `org.shiftsoft.gbibethel`, so Shiftsoft built it). It lists branches in Bandung, Kelapa Gading, Kedoya, Bekasi and Tasikmalaya. — [Google Play: GBI Bethel](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.shiftsoft.gbibethel&hl=en_US)
- **Sinode GBI** is an app for synod officials and regional boards (BPD), not for congregation members. — [Google Play: Sinode GBI](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.bppgbi.si&hl=en_US)
- **GMS Church**, published by Yayasan Festival Kuasa Allah: schedules, ministries, event registration and "personal achievements" or growth milestones on one screen. — [Google Play: GMS Church](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gms.church&hl=en_US), [App Store: GMS Church](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1494423842)
- **Other congregation apps on Play**: GKY Gerendeng, myGKBJ, GSJS, and a generic app called "Mygereja" (`id.mygereja`). Their details could not be fetched. — [GKY Gerendeng](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.gky.grd&hl=en_US), [myGKBJ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mygkbj.app&hl=id), [GSJS](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gsjs.android.app&hl=en_US), [Mygereja](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=id.mygereja&hl=en_US)

**GRII (Stephen Tong) apps**
- **GRII Pusat** (iOS), by Jiosdev LLC, first released 7 Jul 2025, version 7.0.2.
  - Features: an events calendar (day/week/month/agenda views); registration for Sunday services and events, with a QR-code confirmation used for entry; profile editing (birthdate, gender, contact); **family members managed under one account**; church announcements; and add-to-calendar.
  - Fewer than 1,000 downloads and no ratings yet.
  - These facts come through the mwm.ai aggregator, not GRII. — [mwm.ai: GRII Pusat](https://mwm.ai/apps/grii-pusat/6748234472)
- **GRII Sydney** (iREC Sydney): livestreams, a sermon archive, event notifications and a room-booking link. No membership features are described. — [App Store: GRII Sydney](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1525471552)

**Thesis-built systems for single congregations**
- These include GMIT Bethesda Tarus Tengah (2023), HKI Lubuk Pakam (2025), HKBP Yogyakarta, HKBP Cikarang Kota, HKBP Cengkareng (2024; member registration still on paper) and HKBP Bandung Reformanda (registration by SMS gateway). — [Undana TekMas](https://ejurnal.undana.ac.id/index.php/TekMas/article/view/14227), [ULB HKBP Yogyakarta](https://jurnal.ulb.ac.id/index.php/informatika/article/view/205), [Budi Luhur HKBP Cikarang](https://jom.fti.budiluhur.ac.id/IDEALIS/article/view/3237), [Telkom HKBP Reformanda](https://repository.telkomuniversity.ac.id/pustaka/97052/perancangan-aplikasi-sistem-informasi-gereja-hkbp-bandung-reformanda-resort-bandung-tengah-berbasis-web-dan-sms-gateway.html)
- Before these projects, the congregations kept data in Excel or Word. — [UPR Gereja Betesda Palangka Raya](https://e-journal.upr.ac.id/index.php/jcoms/article/view/22447), [Undana TekMas](https://ejurnal.undana.ac.id/index.php/TekMas/article/view/14227)

### Inferences
- No HKBP-wide or GKI-wide member platform was found. Large synods appear to leave member data to individual congregations.
- Mega-churches (GMS, GBI Bethel, GRII Pusat) put money into the **member-facing app**: events, registration, giving and growth tracking. Small SaaS products put money into the **secretary's register**: family cards, office holders and printed cards.
- Pricing anchor for the local market: roughly Rp0–500k per month, tiered by member count.

### Gaps
- No Play Store ratings or review text were retrieved for any app (fetches failed).
- No official app was found for JKI (Injil Kerajaan), Bethany Surabaya, GKI or HKBP.
- No public evidence shows what system GRII uses internally for its membership register.

## 2. Membership: households (KK), NIK, cards, baptism and sidi, atestasi, branches

### Takeaway
The Indonesian vocabulary is consistent across products and theses. Common records are **Kartu Keluarga Jemaat** (household, with a head of family), **Kartu Anggota Jemaat** (printed member card), the sacraments (**baptis, sidi, nikah**), **atestasi masuk/keluar** (transfer letters in and out), deaths, and a configurable **territorial hierarchy** (klasis/sektor/wilayah/rayon). NIK is seldom advertised. Multi-branch support in the GBI style shows up mainly as branch lists inside an app, not as a documented data model.

### Cited Findings
- GKKAI Surabaya (Wiyung branch) lists these processes to computerize: member records, baptism records, atestasi in, atestasi out, deaths and family data. — [Dinamika repository](https://repository.dinamika.ac.id/id/eprint/1175/2/BAB_I.pdf)
- The GMIT Tarus Tengah system covers members, families, positions, and the sacraments (baptis, sidi, nikah). — [Undana TekMas](https://ejurnal.undana.ac.id/index.php/TekMas/article/view/14227)
- In one study, registration, marriage, sidi and baptism forms still had to be submitted offline to the church secretary, which was the motivation for building the system. — [UPJ eprints](https://eprints.upj.ac.id/id/eprint/8678/8/BAB%20I.pdf)
- Gerejaku.id: family card printing, atestasi stored on the individual record, and klasis/sektor terminology an admin can configure. — [Gerejaku.id](https://gerejaku.id/)
- GerejaSoft prints KAJ/KKJ cards and certificates and supports custom fields. — [GerejaSoft](https://www.gerejasoft.com/about.php)
- Shiftsoft offers a digital kartu jemaat in the app. — [Shiftsoft](https://shiftsoft.id/)
- GRII Pusat app: one login manages family members. — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/grii-pusat/6748234472)
- In Singapore, one app-builder pitch describes an encrypted NRIC (national ID) with role-based visibility: pastors see aggregates, finance sees details, members see only their own record. This is marketing for a builder, not a shipped product. — [RationalGo](https://rationalgo.ai/resources/app-builder-singapore/singapore-church-app-builder)

### Inferences
- A household-first model with a head of family, the kind GRII ChurchApp uses, matches local expectations. Printing a KK-style family card and a member card is a feature buyers expect.
- Admins expect to rename the territorial hierarchy (klasis/sektor/wilayah) because each denomination uses different words.
- Atestasi is treated as an event on a member's record (in or out, with the origin or destination church). No product was found that issues atestasi letters digitally between branches.

### Gaps
- No product page explicitly documents NIK storage, a digital atestasi letter workflow, or branch-to-branch transfers in multi-cabang churches.

## 3. Onboarding, directory, export, WhatsApp vs email

### Takeaway
**WhatsApp is the main outbound channel.** Shiftsoft's "WA Blast" uses the church's own number, and GerejaSoft schedules ministry by WhatsApp. Email is barely mentioned. Onboarding happens either through event or service registration in the app (QR tickets) or still on paper with the secretary. Self-service member directories and data export are rarely advertised.

### Cited Findings
- Shiftsoft "WA Blast": connects the church's WhatsApp number and sends announcements. — [Shiftsoft](https://shiftsoft.id/)
- GerejaSoft: automatic ministry scheduling over WhatsApp, notifications and reminders, and online registration. — [GerejaSoft](https://www.gerejasoft.com/about.php)
- HKBP Reformanda (older): new-member registration by SMS gateway. — [Telkom University](https://repository.telkomuniversity.ac.id/pustaka/97052/perancangan-aplikasi-sistem-informasi-gereja-hkbp-bandung-reformanda-resort-bandung-tengah-berbasis-web-dan-sms-gateway.html)
- GRII Pusat: register for Sunday services, receive a QR confirmation, and use it for entry. — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/grii-pusat/6748234472)
- Attendance by QR, RFID or biometrics is common (GerejaSoft, gerejaDigital.id). — [GerejaSoft](https://www.gerejasoft.com/about.php), [gerejaDigital.id](https://gerejadigital.id/)
- Local e-wallet giving (DANA, LinkAja) is offered, alongside a generic payment gateway. — [Shiftsoft](https://shiftsoft.id/), [GerejaSoft](https://www.gerejasoft.com/about.php)
- HKBP Cengkareng still did member registration on paper as of 2024. — search snippet, [ITN JATI](https://ejournal.itn.ac.id/jati/article/download/18724/9867/)

### Inferences
- WhatsApp OTP and notifications fit local habits. A church's own WhatsApp number used for blasts is a competitive feature.
- QR registration for services is now a familiar habit (it spread after COVID capacity limits).

### Gaps
- No product clearly documents a member-facing directory or CSV/Excel export. They may exist without being advertised.
- No source covered the official WhatsApp Business API compared with unofficial "blast" tools. The ban risk of unofficial tools is not documented here.

## 4. UU PDP No. 27/2022 implications

### Takeaway
Under UU PDP, **religion is general personal data, not specific (sensitive) data**. Church registers still hold specific data: **children's data**, and often health, financial (giving) or biometric attendance data. Those raise duties such as DPIAs and possibly a DPO. The law's 2-year transition ended in October 2024. No church-specific guidance and no religious-organisation exemption was found.

### Cited Findings
- Article 4 splits data into specific data (health, biometric, genetic, criminal record, **child data**, personal financial data, and others) and general data (name, gender, nationality, **religion**, marital status, and combinations that identify a person). Processing specific data is high risk and can trigger a DPIA. Large-scale processing of specific data can require a DPO. — [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/perbedaan-jenis-data-spesifik-vs-umum-dalam-uu-pelindungan-data-pribadi-lt633f548091bfe)
- Indonesian press reported in October 2024 that UU PDP is fully in force, covering the consequences for data leaks and the delayed creation of the supervisory agency (lembaga PDP). — [detik: UU PDP sudah berlaku](https://inet.detik.com/law-and-policy/d-7597590/uu-pdp-sudah-berlaku-ini-dampak-pelanggar-kebocoran-data), [Hukumonline: UU PDP berlaku](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/uu-pdp-berlaku--appdi-bakal-perkuat-praktisi-pelindungan-data-pribadi-lt6719e9bd2c1ca), [Bisnis: implementasi mundur](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20241014/84/1807455/implementasi-uu-pdp-mundur-setelah-pelantikan-prabowo-gibran)
- Indonesian police warned about Play Store apps branded as religious that harvested data (clipboard, GPS) through third-party SDKs. The warning is undated and names no app. — [INP Polri](https://inp.polri.go.id/artikel/the-authorities-warned-about-the-data-theft-under-the-guise-of-religious-application)
- Local vendors make only vague security claims ("aman"). — [e-Gereja](https://www.e-gereja.com/)
- Singapore comparison: churches publish PDPA notices. CMC Trellis and KY CMS run on the Sojourner platform, hosted in Singapore, to meet local data protection rules. The PDPC has published guidance that covers a religious institution. — [CMC Trellis](https://www.cmc.org.sg/trellis/), [Kum Yan CMS](https://kumyan.org.sg/connect/cms/), [CMC PDPA notice](https://cmc.org.sg/pdpa), [PDPC guidance](https://pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/files/pdpc/pdf-files/practical-guidance-provided-by-pdpc/guidance-religious-institution-and-retail-chain.pdf)

### Inferences
- For GRII ChurchApp, the following need extra care: children in households (specific data), NIK (identifying data that can be misused), giving records (financial) and counseling notes. The project's existing rules already cover part of this: NIK masked in the audit log, pastoral notes never sent to external services, and soft delete. Adding consent records and a data-subject access or export path would support UU PDP rights. This is an inference, not legal advice.

### Gaps
- No source was found on whether the PDP supervisory agency was operational by 2026, or on its implementing regulation (RPP PDP).
- No church-specific Indonesian guidance was found.

## 5. Chinese-language, bilingual and regional products

### Takeaway
No Indonesian product advertised Chinese or multilingual UI. The Chinese-heritage congregations found (GKY, GRII) publish Indonesian-first apps. Elsewhere, Hong Kong has ITChurch (iGears), and Singapore churches mostly use global SaaS plus a few local platforms. No local Korean or Taiwanese products were identified in this research.

### Cited Findings
- **Hong Kong**: ITChurch, a subsidiary of iGears, builds church websites, a CMS, a church management system (the "CIS" member and event system) and church apps. — [iGears ITChurch](https://igears.net/eng/it_church)
- **Singapore**:
  - Sojourner Church Management Platform, hosted in Singapore, runs CMC Trellis and Kum Yan KY CMS. — [CMC Trellis](https://www.cmc.org.sg/trellis/), [Kum Yan](https://kumyan.org.sg/connect/cms/)
  - MosesTab markets a "PDPA-compliant" member database. — [MosesTab](https://mosestab.com/for/singapore)
  - Directories list mostly global tools (Tithe.ly 4.7/5 from 693 reviews, Breeze, Planning Center, ChurchSuite, Servant Keeper) plus Church on Cloud. — [GetApp SG](https://www.getapp.sg/directory/166/church-management/software), [Capterra SG](https://www.capterra.com.sg/directory/20046/church-management/software), [SoftwareSuggest SG](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/church-management-software/singapore)
- **Malaysia, Korea, Taiwan, Philippines**: searches returned only global market reports or directories. No named local product was found. — [GII Korea report](https://giikorea.co.kr/report/sky1914104-church-management-software-market-size-share.html)

### Inferences
- A trilingual or quadrilingual UI (id/en/zh-Hans/zh-Hant), plus machine-translated warta, appears to be **unserved** by the Indonesian products found. This is a possible differentiator for GRII ChurchApp, whose congregations include Mandarin services.

### Gaps
- Korean ChMS vendors (교적 관리) were not identified. Korean-language queries returned only market reports, so a native-language source check is needed.
- Taiwan and Philippines products were not found.
- No source documented how Chinese names (Chinese characters alongside an Indonesian legal name) are stored.

## 6. Reported strengths and weaknesses

### Takeaway
Little independent review data was found. Strengths and weaknesses below are inferred from feature pages and theses.

### Cited Findings
- Strength claimed by theses: moving from Excel or paper to a web system improved recording, search and report preparation (HKI Lubuk Pakam, 2025). — search summary citing [Universitas Pahlawan journal](https://journal.universitaspahlawan.ac.id/index.php/cdj/article/download/55238/33664)
- Weaknesses of manual data: inaccurate records and inefficient operations (HKBP Cikarang). — [Budi Luhur](https://jom.fti.budiluhur.ac.id/IDEALIS/article/view/3237)
- Weakness in local SaaS: security is described only in vague terms, with no detail on encryption, backups or hosting location. — [e-Gereja](https://www.e-gereja.com/)
- The GRII Pusat app has no ratings and fewer than 1,000 downloads, so adoption looks low or very recent. — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/grii-pusat/6748234472)

### Inferences
- Local SaaS strengths: low rupiah prices, a free tier, the Indonesian church vocabulary (KK, sidi, atestasi, klasis), and WhatsApp.
- Weaknesses seen: register-only products, or app-only products without a solid register; no multilingual support; little documentation of branch scoping or export; weak privacy disclosures.

### Gaps
- No Play Store or App Store review text and no forum discussions (Kaskus) were retrieved. A browser-based review scrape is needed for real user complaints.
